# SRS V1

The V1 SRS is deliberately deterministic, explainable and versioned. It is not
FSRS.

## States

```text
new -> learning -> review
                  |
                  v
              relearning
                  |
                  v
                review

review/relearning may be marked leech
```

User-owned states (known-already, suspended, buried) are independent of the SRS
state machine.

## Short learning steps

Default learning steps: **1, 10, 60 minutes**.

Default relearning steps: **10, 60 minutes**.

A verified failure resets/enters the appropriate short-step sequence. Graduation
hands the card to the long-review policy.

## Long-review intervals

Policy version 1 base intervals:

| Box | Base interval |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 8 hours |
| 2 | 1 day |
| 3 | 3 days |
| 4 | 7 days |
| 5 | 14 days |
| 6 | 30 days |
| 7 | 60 days |

Intervals are adjusted deterministically by bounded difficulty, elapsed-time
evidence and deterministic jitter (default ±10%). Verified relapse applies a box
penalty before relearning.

## Signals

The SRS does not treat every interaction as equal evidence.

- exposure: no retrieval evidence;
- post-retrieval self-assessment: weak, never enough to bypass verified gate;
- verified MCQ: medium;
- verified free-text/cloze/exam: strong;
- hint use reduces evidence quality;
- untrusted shared-device interactions are reduced/unverified;
- `unrecognized` grading is neutral, not an automatic wrong answer.

A positive self-assessment made after the answer was already visible cannot
promote the card.

## Verified gate

Higher-confidence progression requires periodic trusted verified retrieval. The
signal policy persists signal quality/version in ReviewEvents so future policy
changes can be analyzed.

## Mastery

Mastery is a secondary, non-terminal indicator derived from state/evidence and
decays with elapsed time. It is not a permanent learned/not-learned flag.

## Leech policy

Policy V1 marks a card as leech when either condition holds inside the versioned
window:

- at least **6 failures among the most recent 10 trusted verified attempts**; or
- at least **8 verified relapses in 60 days**.

Leech status reduces normal exposure frequency and directs remediation toward
targeted sessions/annotations rather than silently deleting the card.

## Sibling/confusable controls

Selection prevents unsafe introduction/review clustering using LearningItem sibling
identity, prerequisites and PackVersion confusable-group minimum gaps.

## Undo and rebuild

ReviewEvents retain complete pre/post Progress snapshots. Undo appends compensating
history rather than deleting evidence. Admin rebuild/recompute operations are
separate and fail closed when historical semantics cannot be replayed safely.

## Metacognitive calibration

Known-already declarations are user state, not fabricated SRS success. Calibration
later compares those declarations against trusted verified retrieval.

## Future FSRS

FSRS is a roadmap candidate, not V1 behavior. Any migration must preserve
ReviewEvent history and version policy semantics rather than rewriting past
evidence as if it had been generated by FSRS.
